Auction Terms & Details

Housing Design Auction Terms & Details

What the auction includes, what happens if the threshold is not met, and how the process works if a custom design is unlocked.

What the Auction Includes (Guaranteed for the Winner)

Bid process starts at 10m gil for both Option A or Option B.

The auction winner will receive ONE of the following at no additional cost:

Option A

A fully custom designed Apartment build, delivered as:

  • A MakePlace file
  • A complete item list for easy in-game implementation

Option B

A premade housing build (listed below), delivered as:

  • A MakePlace file
  • A complete item list for easy in-game implementation

This format ensures the winning bidder walks away with a complete, usable design regardless of bid amount.

Threshold Note

If the threshold is not met

If the threshold is not met, the winner will still receive:

The included apartment OR premade MakePlace build (winner's choice)

Bid with confidence. Every winner leaves with a complete design.

Priority Placement

Priority placement for auction winners

While I maintain an active client queue, auction winners requesting a custom design will be placed on a priority list.

  • Priority does not guarantee immediate completion.
  • It does ensure faster consultation scheduling.
  • It also allows earlier draft placement than standard commissions.

Quality and detail remain the top priority for every build.

Payment & Process

If custom design is unlocked

  1. 01

    Auction results

    Auction results will determine what size home is available for custom design. We will discuss what size or type of home design you would like.

  2. 02

    Draft phase

    MakePlace draft images provided.

    Major edits, layout changes, and stylistic feedback encouraged.

    Note: MakePlace lighting and colors may differ slightly from in-game results.

  3. 03

    Final approval

    Once the direction is approved, the build moves to final implementation.

  4. 04

    Implementation

    You can get the items on your own, but you will need to allow me access to the home so I can place them and take photos after the design is finished. No further gil is required with this method.

    You will need to provide additional gil for required housing items if I am getting the items for you. Estimated pricing will be discussed during the draft phase.

    Build is completed in-game.

For full custom commission terms, payment structure, and queue details, use the process page.
